Color

Gotcha: the red accent kept its original dark-theme value (#E80505 passes WCAG AA on white at ~4.71:1). Any element sitting on the red accent or --cta-gradient must pin its text to --cta-fg (hardcoded white) — never --ink, which is now dark and renders unreadable on red.

03

Spacing

The "Bay Hardware" scale — named after garage fasteners and structures, smallest to largest. Exposed as Tailwind spacing utilities (e.g. p-panel, gap-bay).

04

Border Radius

Five steps. Corners stay tight and mechanical — nothing softer than 12px except fully-round pills and avatars.

05

Elevation

Shadow alphas were softened for the light ground — the original dark-theme values read as heavy on white. The red glow is reserved for accent/CTA surfaces.
